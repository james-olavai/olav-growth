Most companies spend 80% of their time building and 20% distributing.

It should be the exact opposite.

Over the last 6 months, we tested 14 different publishing workflows across 5 platforms. Here are 3 hard-learned lessons that changed everything:

1. Consistency beats intensity every time.
Batching content into structured campaigns prevents the dreaded "what do I post today?" paralysis.

2. Repurpose with native formatting, not copy-paste.
A 3:4 visual deck works on Xiaohongshu, Instagram, and Pinterest. But your captions must adapt to the platform's native dialect.

3. Build once, distribute automatically.
Centralizing your content vault under Markdown + Git allows multi-agent pipelines to handle formatting and scheduling.
