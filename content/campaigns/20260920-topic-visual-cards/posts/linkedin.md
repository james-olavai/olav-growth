Most teams using AI for visual marketing make the same fatal mistake:

They ask the model for "stunning visuals" instead of "information density".

The result? Glossy, generic gradient posters that look like stock photos. No one reads them. No one saves them.

Over the last 3 months, we tested 50+ carousel frameworks across LinkedIn, XHS, and Instagram. Here are 3 hard rules we now enforce:

1. The 3:4 Aspect Ratio Rule
Stop designing custom canvas sizes for every channel. Standardize on 3:4 (1080x1440). It works natively across Xiaohongshu, Instagram carousels, and Pinterest pins without losing visual punch.

2. Information Architecture Over Art
Your first slide has exactly 1.5 seconds to stop the scroll. Bold typography, high-contrast dark mode, and a specific counter-intuitive premise beat fancy illustrations every single time.

3. Strip the Corporate Grease
Nobody trusts third-person buzzwords like "empowering seamless ecosystems". Speak founder-to-founder. Share the actual architecture and the numbers.
